Episode III — About That Blue Dot

Phrancis recognized the map by the blue dot.
A student worker had dragged it into the pond during a rehearsal, and everyone had laughed.
They had fixed the map before printing the real handouts.
That had been last spring.

The open house was at one o’clock.
The chancellor planned to welcome visitors in the library.
Phrancis had spent two weeks getting the volunteers, signs, and handouts in the right places.
Now the admissions staff wanted to know which map to give people.

“Give them the one that keeps their shoes dry,” she said.

Phred asked why a map from last spring would show up today.
Phrancis forwarded him both versions and went back to work.
The old version was called PREVIEW.
The current version had a date in its filename.

Someone in admissions had already put a stack of previews by the front door.
