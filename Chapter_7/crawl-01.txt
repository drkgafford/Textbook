Episode I — The Printer Strikes First

On his first morning at Pharaday University, Phred reported to the IT department fifteen minutes early.
His wife, Phrancis, had worked at Pharaday long enough to know that if someone caught him in the hallway with a quick question, he’d need those fifteen minutes.

His brother Phrank, the IT manager, handed him a badge and pointed him toward a desk.
His sister Phranie had also been hired to help.
She was already at the desk next to his, reading the first ticket of the day.

The library printer was printing the same page over and over.
The librarian had pulled the paper tray out to make it stop.
Then someone put more paper in.

Phrank handed Phred the ticket. “Start with this one. It’s just a printer.”

Phranie turned her monitor toward him.
The print job had come from a computer the university retired last spring.

“Welcome to Pharaday,” she said.
