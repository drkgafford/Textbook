Episode IV — The One-Minute Quiet

Phred paused the library queue and cleared the test jobs that were still waiting.
The printer finally went quiet.
He left a note on it so the librarian would know why her next job was waiting.

Phranie watched the log from her desk.
At 9:46, another copy of PREVIEW arrived in the library queue.
At 9:47, another arrived.
The science building and admissions were still getting theirs.

“If this were one stuck job, I’d expect to keep seeing the same job number,” she said.
“Look at these.”

Each minute brought three new job numbers.
Phranie saved the matching lines and added a note to the ticket.

Source: LIB-DESK-04
Interval: 1 minute
Checked by: Phranie

Phred looked toward the library.
He had bought them some quiet.
He still owed the librarian a printer.
