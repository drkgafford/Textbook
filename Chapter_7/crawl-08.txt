Episode VIII — The Last Bad Map

The volunteers found most of the old maps before the doors opened.
Phrancis put a current copy at each welcome table and asked the volunteers to look for the date in the lower right corner.

One old map made it out.
A visitor brought it back to the library and asked whether the duck pond was part of the tour.
He had already taken three photographs and wanted to know where the rest of the group had gone.

Phred gave him the new map.
The visitor folded the old one and put it in his pocket.

“I’m keeping this one,” he said. “It was the only map that told me exactly where I was.”

At one o’clock, the chancellor welcomed the visitors.
All three printers were quiet.
