Episode V — Retired, With Benefits

The asset register said LIB-DESK-04 had been removed from the library in May.
Phrank remembered carrying it out himself.
He remembered it because the elevator was out and it was a very long walk to the loading dock.

Phranie opened the retirement ticket.
The last entry said a copy of the machine had been saved as a virtual machine so the team could retrieve an old label-printing template.
There was no follow-up entry saying anyone had removed the copy.

Phred walked over to Phrank’s office with the ticket.

“Did you retire the computer?”

“Yes.”

“Did anybody tell the computer?”

Phrank pulled up the list of virtual machines.
LIB-DESK-04 was listed as running.
It had started at eight that morning.
