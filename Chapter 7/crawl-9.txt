Episode IX — Before You Close the Ticket

After the open house, Phrank asked for a summary he could send to the help desk.
Phred opened the original ticket and started to write that the printer had malfunctioned.
Phranie read the first line over his shoulder.

“The printer did everything we asked it to do,” she said.

Phred tried again.

Cause: A scheduled task on LIB-DESK-04 submitted a preview map every minute.
Affected locations: Library, Science, Admissions
Fix: Disabled OPEN-HOUSE-PREVIEW, removed queued previews, and verified normal printing.
Follow-up: Review scheduled tasks before bringing retired systems back online.
Closed by: Phred

Phrancis stopped by on her way out.
She put a clean campus map on Phred’s desk and circled the IT office.

“For tomorrow,” she said. “You’ll want to get here fifteen minutes early.”
