Episode II — Three Buildings, One Job

By nine o’clock, the library had company.
The science building called to report an extra page coming out of its printer every minute.
The admissions office called a minute later.
They asked whether the extra page was part of the open house.

Phranie pulled the print server log and found the same document going to all three buildings.
The name of the document was PREVIEW.
The account that sent it was printcheck.
The computer listed as the source was LIB-DESK-04.

Phred picked up the library’s copy.
It was a campus map with a large blue dot in the middle of the duck pond.
Under the dot, in letters big enough to read from across the room, were the words YOU ARE HERE.

“Only if you’ve had a very bad morning,” said Phranie.

Phrank opened the ticket and typed one more line.
